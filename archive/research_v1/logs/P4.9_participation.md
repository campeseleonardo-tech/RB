# P4.9_participation - fragmented participation and size data per country

## Countries processed: AT, DE, IT, ES, FR, NL, PL, CH (+ EU level)

## Research Method
Searching for:
1. National physical-activity/sport surveys that EXPLICITLY name dance/dancing as an activity
2. Eurostat culture-accounts note: 'cult_pcs_*' tables
3. Licence/membership statistics from federations
4. French dance license data (300,000+ licence holders from D7)

## Summary
This research systematically collected dance participation data from public sources across 8 European countries and EU level, following the fragmented approach described in the quarantine D7 file to replace the problematic "authoritative per-capita study" claims.
