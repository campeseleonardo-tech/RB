## Summary (max 200 words)

Red Bull Athlete Performance Center operates two sites (Salzburg, Austria and Los Angeles, USA) and supports 900+ athletes across 200+ disciplines via 6 service areas: physical training, sports medicine, mental performance, profiling, nutrition, and recovery. Access is professional‑only and invitation‑based; no public selection criteria or salary information is disclosed. Red Bull Basement is a student‑focused innovation programme (university students 18+, early‑stage founders) with AI‑accelerated development, DIY‑based technological solutions, and a global World Final (San Francisco, June 3‑4 2026). Red Bull Next Generation is a regional athlete development programme launched 10 June 2026 in Malaysia and Indonesia, targeting athletes aged 15‑25 (Indonesian/Malaysian) across all sports and disciplines, including esports; it provides expert guidance, practical support (performance, nutrition, recovery, mindset, media, personal branding), workshops, mentorship, and visibility, with applications open June 2026 and selections announced from August 2026. The programme is not yet active in Europe, with plans to expand across South East Asia. Athlete contracts with Red Bull provide services but not confirmed salary; Red Bull gains brand exposure via its global athlete portal. WDSF national federations (Germany DTV, Italy FIDS, Spain FEBD) operate Breaking talent pathways with national squads and competition pipelines, but these are limited to WDSF‑registered athletes and do not cover social‑dance scenes. Nike and Adidas have no published dance‑specific scholarship or residency programmes for 2026. Key gap: no public sport‑or culture‑funding pathways exist for hip hop/Latin dancers outside elite WDSF competition structures and the new Red Bull Next Generation regional platform (currently limited to SE Asia).
