# I4 — Ten validation questions for Red Bull contacts (P2)

## Overview
Draft questions to validate the footprint inventory and scouting hypotheses with Red Bull contacts. Grounded in A1 (Red Bull events inventory) and A3 (Caribbean/Latin dance presence), all answerable from general knowledge/role, suitable for 30-minute conversation.

## Validation Questions

### 1. Red Bull dance format dominance
**"Red Bull's dance portfolio focuses almost exclusively on breaking (BC One) and mixed-style Dance Your Style, with minimal dedicated presence in traditional Latin dance formats like salsa/bachata competitions or training programs. Is this your understanding of their current strategy?"
*Why we ask:* Validates A3 finding that Red Bull's Caribbean/Latin presence is incidental and confirms focus on hip hop/breaking.

### 2. BC One global expansion
**"BC One has grown from regional qualifiers in ~20 cities (2004-2005) to a global 27-city tour in 2026 across five continents. Does this expansion pattern align with your knowledge of Red Bull's geographical strategy?"
*Why we ask:* Confirms A1 data on BC One growth and validates expansion hypothesis.

### 3. Dance Your Style versus BC One positioning
**"Dance Your Style serves as Red Bull's crowd-voted, mixed-style competition (including incidental Latin elements), while BC One targets pure 1v1 breaking excellence. How do these two formats complement Red Bull's overall dance ecosystem?"
*Why we ask:* Validates distinction between formats and confirms complementary roles.

### 4. Future Latin dance expansion interest
**"Given that Red Bull's current Latin dance presence is limited to incidental inclusion in Dance Your Style and the historic Breakin' Salsa 2012 event, what factors would make Red Bull consider dedicated salsa/bachata programming in key markets?"
*Why we ask:* Tests hypothesis about potential expansion based on market gaps.

### 5. Scene strength correlation with Red Bull presence
**"You see strong Red Bull BC One scenes in cities like Toronto, Berlin, Paris, and Tokyo. Do you observe a correlation between Red Bull event frequency and overall local breaking scene activity in these markets?"
*Why we ask:* Begins validation of J2 calibration hypothesis about Red Bull events potentially causing scene growth.

### 6. Non-Red Bull event ecosystem
**"Major non-Red Bull events dominate: Juste Debout (>10,000 participants, Paris), Battle of the Year (Germany-based), IBE (Heerlen), Outbreak Europe (Slovakia). How does Red Bull position itself relative to these established competitors?"
*Why we ask:* Validates B1 data on competitor landscape and Red Bull's positioning.

### 7. Athlete mobility and Red Bull influence
**"Red Bull athletes like Victor (USA) and India (Netherlands) move between cities and countries post-BC One success. Does Red Bull's athlete mobility contribute to scene development in new markets?"
*Why we ask:* Tests export/import hypothesis from A5 athlete programme insights.

### 8. Latin dance market gaps
**"In markets like Austria, Germany, Italy, Spain, you see strong salsa/bachata scenes but minimal Red Bull presence. What barriers exist to Red Bull engaging with these established Latin dance communities?"
*Why we ask:* Explores opportunity for Red Bull expansion into Caribbean/Latin markets.

### 9. National qualifier ecosystem
**"BC One qualifiers serve as national selection (e.g., BC One Cypher Austria, Germany, Spain, Italy). How effective are these qualifiers at identifying and nurturing new talent for the World Final?"
*Why we ask:* Validates entry route methodology from A4.

### 10. Scene strength measurement validation
**"You track scene strength through multiple indicators: event frequency, athlete participation, online engagement, and content volume. How would you validate a composite scene-strength score across disciplines like breaking versus salsa/bachata?"
*Why we ask:* Directly tests J1 scoring methodology approach.

## Conversation Flow

**Easy → Harder progression:**
1-3: Fact validation (Red Bull's current strategy, format understanding)
4-5: Strategy understanding (expansion, correlation)
6-7: Ecosystem analysis (competitors, athlete impact)
8-9: Opportunity analysis (market gaps, qualifier effectiveness)
10: Methodology validation (scoring approach)

## Conversation Design Notes

- All questions avoid asking for confidential information
- Focus on observable facts and general industry knowledge
- Each question builds on previous findings from the research
- Designed to validate both existing data and test new hypotheses
- Leave room for Red Bull contacts to share insights beyond the research scope
