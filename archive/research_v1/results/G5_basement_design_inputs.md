## Design parameters (5–8), grounded in the programmes found

| # | parameter | proposed value | grounded in |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cohort size | 30–60 per national edition | Red Bull Next Generation runs "in selected markets" with local delivery; APC is selective (900 athletes across 200+ disciplines); EIC funds 61 companies in a single round — 30–60 allows per-athlete mentoring while keeping a national final viable |
| 2 | Duration | 9–12 months, split into 3 phases (local qualifiers → national final/bootcamp → development) | Mirrors Red Bull Basement's multi-phase structure (Phase 1 qualifiers, Phase 2 National Finals, Phase 3 Development, then World Final) and its 3-week accelerator for the winner |
| 3 | Selection criteria | Portfolio + "mindset/attitude" judgement, not results only | Red Bull Next Generation FAQ: "Selection goes beyond results… what drives you, how you approach your sport" |
| 4 | Funding per participant | €2,000–€5,000 non-contingent grant, plus a €50,000–€100,000 equity-free prize at the final | Red Bull Basement World Final 2026: USD 100,000 equity-free; EIC comparator: grants up to €4M, 70% funding rate, blended grant/equity at 61 awards per round |
| 5 | Curriculum | 4 pillars: physical performance & injury prevention; nutrition & recovery; mental performance; business/media & monetisation (social, content, sponsorship) | APC service pillars (performance training, sports medicine/physio, nutrition, recovery, mental performance & lifestyling) + APC's "performance lifestyling" and "athlete development" use cases; Red Bull Next Generation adds "media" to performance/nutrition/mindset |
| 6 | Mentoring format | 1:1 long-term mentor + peer cohort + expert masterclasses in person at the final | Red Bull Basement: 4 days of workshops, lectures and mentorship; "3 Tour Stops" on the World Final route; APC's tailored individual support |
| 7 | Deliverables per participant | 1 personal performance/business plan, 1 funded production or competition appearance, 1 income-diversification plan (teaching + performance + content), 1 injury-prevention protocol, monthly check-ins | EIC/EIT/accelerator logic: 24-month project with milestones; EuroFIA finding that dancers need self-financed training and transition support; Dance/NYC: 54% of dance workers also work outside the industry |
| 8 | Physical infrastructure | Use existing regional hubs rather than new build: Salzburg APC (Austria) as the anchor, plus satellite studio partnerships in Berlin, Milan, Barcelona, Paris, Amsterdam | APC is already located in Salzburg and LA; Dance/NYC notes 64% of workers pay out of pocket for their own work — infrastructure access is a documented gap a programme can absorb |
| 9 | Evaluation | Track: retention in dance at 12/24 months, share of participants with ≥2 income streams, teaching hours and earnings change, injury rate, progression to national/international competition | Red Bull Next Generation is new with no published results; APC reports only "thousands of athlete visits" without outcome data — a baseline measurement design is itself a design requirement. Not found: published survival/progression rates for any Red Bull talent programme |

## Summary (≤200 words)

Red Bull already runs two transferable templates. Basement is a multi-phase, application-based global incubator (50+ countries): local qualifiers → National Finals → Development phase → World Final (San Francisco 2026, 3 Tour Stops), USD 100,000 equity-free prize, 3-week LA/Silicon Valley accelerator for the winner, and a University edition running 4 days of workshops, lectures and mentorship. APC is the "athlete accelerator" model: established 2019, 900+ athletes across 200+ disciplines, six service pillars (performance training, sports medicine, nutrition, recovery, mental performance, assessment), already anchored in Salzburg and Los Angeles. Red Bull Next Generation is the closest to a talent programme: launched first in Indonesia and Malaysia, selecting on mindset rather than results and providing financial support for training, travel and competition, with performance, nutrition, mindset and media curriculum — but no results published yet.

Measured outcomes are the gap: no published survival or progression rates for any Red Bull talent programme; APC reports only "thousands of athlete visits". EIC Accelerator (61 SMEs, €467M, 85% blended) is the only comparator with published funding mechanics. Design parameters (9, above) are derived from these structures plus documented dancer gaps: self-financed training, 54% multi-jobbing, 64% paying out of pocket for their own work.
