## SUMMARY (max 200 words)

Red Bull's dance portfolio for 2024-2026 is dominated by two series: BC One (breaking, 1v1) and Dance Your Style (mixed-style, crowd-voted). The 2026 BC One tour comprised 27 events across five continents, with regional qualifiers in Kazakhstan, Spain, Germany, Netherlands, USA (Denver, Seattle), UK (London), Switzerland (Lausanne), Belgium (Ghent), Ukraine (Kyiv), Romania (Bucharest), Italy (L'Aquila), India, Taiwan, Colombia, Thailand (Bangkok, two stops), Canada, South Africa, France (Paris/Aubervilliers), Australia, Morocco, and the World Final in Toronto (Nov 29). Confidence is mostly Low (L) due to JS-heavy redbull.com pages, with Medium (M) for Austrian and Belgian qualifiers confirmed via and8.dance and local sources. Dance Your Style 2026 had 24+ national finals across six continents, with the World Final in Zurich (Oct 24). Confirmed national finals include USA (Tampa, Sep 18-19), Australia (Sydney, Jul 25, ~4,000 attendees), Philippines (Manila, Apr 11), Brazil (São Paulo, May 2), Kenya (Nairobi, May 23), New Zealand (Auckland, Apr 18), Nigeria (Lagos, Jun 28), Germany (Hamburg, Aug 2), Austria (Vienna, Aug 28), Finland (Helsinki+Oulu, Sep 5), Portugal (Lisbon, Sep 5), France (Montpellier, Sep 26), Georgia (Tbilisi, Jul 12), Kazakhstan (Karaganda, Aug 30), Mauritius (Sep 26), and UK (London, May 30). Historical world finals: BC One 2024 Rio, 2025 Tokyo; DYS 2024 Mumbai, 2025 Los Angeles. Known issues resolved: BC One 2022 was New York (not Johannesburg); DYS 2021 was cancelled (not Johannesburg); DYS 2020 was Online Challenge virtual.
