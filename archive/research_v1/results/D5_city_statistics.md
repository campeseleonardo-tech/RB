## Definitional inconsistencies between countries

| Issue | Countries affected | Explanation |
|-------|-------------------|-------------|
| **City proper definition** | All | AT/DE/ES/IT/NL/FR use municipal boundaries (Gemeinde/kommune/comune/municipio/gemeente/commune). These differ in physical extent (e.g., Milan municipality small vs. greater city large; Vienna is both city and state). |
| **Metro / FUA definition** | All | Eurostat provides **Functional Urban Area (FUA)** = city + commuting zone (≥15% commute). ESPON FUA uses similar but not identical methodology. Some sources (Wikipedia table) cite Eurostat FUA, others ESPON, others "metropolitan region" (NUTS3-based). **Not comparable without harmonisation.** |
| **Population register basis** | AT vs others | Austria uses *registered main residence* (Hauptwohnsitz), not usual residence. Others use usual residence or census. |
| **Age bands** | All | Eurostat urb_cpop1 provides 5-year bands. **15-34 share is not a standard published indicator**; must be computed from bands. Most city statistical yearbooks publish broad groups (0-14, 15-64, 65+). |
| **Students** | DE, AT | Eurostat urb_ceduc "students per 1000 residents" exists for some cities (e.g., Munich 74). Not available for all. National definitions of "student" differ (enrolled vs. degree-seeking). |
| **Tourist nights** | All | Eurostat urb_ctour exists but not retrieved for these cities in this session. National tourism boards use different methodologies (hotel nights vs. all accommodation). |
| **GDP / income** | All | Eurostat metropolitan GDP (met_10r_3gdp) is at NUTS3 level, not city/FUA. "GDP per head" often refers to NUTS3 region (e.g., Salzburg AT32, Innsbruck AT332). Not comparable to city proper. |
| **Reference years** | All | Latest common year varies: population 2025 (Eurostat urb_cpop1), FUA 2021–2022, GDP 2021–2023, students 2023. No single harmonised year. |

## Summary (≤200 words)

Official city-level statistics are available for population (city proper) for all 11 primary cities via national statistical offices and Eurostat Urban Audit (urb_cpop1, latest 2025). Metropolitan/FUA figures exist from Eurostat (urb_lpop1, met_pjangrp3) but reference years differ (2021–2022) and definitions vary (FUA vs. NUTS3 vs. ESPON). The 15–34 age share is **not published as a standard indicator** in any accessed source; only broad bands (0–14, 15–64, 65+) or 5-year bands in raw Eurostat tables (requiring manual aggregation). University student counts are sparse: Vienna (192,699, WS 2022/23) and Munich (74/1000 residents, 2023) are the only ones found. Tourist overnight stays were not retrieved from Eurostat urb_ctour in this session. Income/GDP per head is available only at NUTS3 regional level (e.g., Salzburg 89,500 PPS GDP/person employed 2021; Innsbruck €55,800 GDP/capita 2023; Vienna €56,600 GRP/capita 2022), not at city/FUA level. **No fully harmonised cross-city dataset exists for all requested indicators at a common reference year.** Definitional flags: Austria uses registered residence; FUA definitions differ; student and tourism methodologies vary.
