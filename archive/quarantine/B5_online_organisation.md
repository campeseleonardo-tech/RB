## Summary (≤200 words)
Dance scenes across Europe (AT, DE, IT, ES, NL, FR) utilize mixed online organization models combining public platforms (Meetup, public Facebook groups, Instagram hashtags) with private coordination (WhatsApp, Facebook groups). While public group memberships remain opaque due to platform restrictions, the pattern shows consistent community organization across disciplines with stronger presence in street dance (breaking) than Latin dance (salsa/bachata). Event calendars and dedicated portals provide measurable activity indicators, though direct community size metrics remain inaccessible. The key limitation is platform privacy policies preventing automated data collection, requiring reliance on manual observation and public event listings for community activity estimation.
