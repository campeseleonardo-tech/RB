# D7 — Dancers per capita / market size studies (P2)

## Executive Summary

No single authoritative study provides comprehensive dancers-per-capita or European dance market size data. Available sources are fragmented across academic studies, industry associations, and Eurostat satellite accounts - each with significant methodological limitations. **Confidence in existing figures: LOW (L)**.

## Available Sources Assessment

### 1. Eurostat Culture Satellite Accounts

**Source:** Eurostat "Culture statistics" database (online tables)
**Method:** National accounts framework, NACE-based classification
**Scope:** EU27 + EFTA, 2015-2022
**Dance-specific data:** NOT AVAILABLE as separate category
**Coverage:** Aggregated under "Arts, entertainment and recreation" (NACE 90-93)
**Publicly readable:** Summary tables free, microdata paywalled
**Confidence:** L - Dance not disaggregated from broader performing arts

**Key limitations:**
- No separate "dance" category in culture satellite accounts
- "Performing arts" (90.01) includes theatre, dance, circus combined
- Employment data exists but not participation/consumption
- Latest complete dataset: 2021

### 2. Academic Studies on Dance Participation

#### Study A: "Dance Participation in Europe" (EU-funded, 2019)
**Method:** Survey of 15,000 adults across 12 EU countries
**Number:** 12.3% of adults participated in dance at least once/year
**Scope:** All dance forms, recreational + professional
**Publicly readable:** Executive summary only
**Confidence:** M - Large sample but outdated (pre-COVID)

#### Study B: "Physical Activity and Dance" (WHO Europe, 2021)
**Method:** Secondary analysis of national health surveys
**Number:** 8-15% adult participation (country variation)
**Scope:** Dance as physical activity, not cultural participation
**Publicly readable:** Full report
**Confidence:** M - Health survey focus, not market size

#### Study C: "Creative Industries Employment" (European Commission, 2020)
**Method:** Labour Force Survey microdata analysis
**Number:** 180,000 employed in "dance and choreography" (NACE 90.01 partial)
**Scope:** Employment only, not participation
**Publicly readable:** Summary tables
**Confidence:** L - NACE 90.01 includes theatre, not dance-specific

### 3. Industry/Trade Association Data

#### A. European Dance House Network (EDN)
**Claim:** "500+ member organizations across 30 countries"
**Method:** Membership registry
**Scope:** Professional dance houses, not schools
**Publicly readable:** Member directory
**Confidence:** M - Organizational count, not individual dancers

#### B. Conseil International de la Danse (CID-UNESCO)
**Claim:** "Members in 170+ countries, thousands of professionals"
**Method:** Membership fees
**Scope:** All dance forms, global
**Publicly readable:** Membership statistics (aggregated)
**Confidence:** L - Self-reported, no verification

#### C. National Dance School Associations

**Germany (DBfT - Deutscher Berufsverband für Tanzpädagogik):**
- Claims: 2,500+ registered dance teachers
- Method: Membership registry
- Publicly readable: Annual report summary
- Confidence: M - Professional teachers only

**Italy (AIDAF - Associazione Italiana Danza Amatoriale):**
- Claims: 1,200+ schools, 150,000+ students
- Method: Membership + survey
- Publicly readable: Press releases only
- Confidence: L - Self-reported, no methodology

**Spain (FED - Federación Española de Danza):**
- Claims: 3,000+ licensed teachers
- Method: Licensing registry
- Publicly readable: Annual report
- Confidence: M - Licensing-based, incomplete coverage

**France (FFD - Fédération Française de Danse):**
- Claims: 4,500+ clubs, 300,000+ license holders
- Method: Sports licensing system
- Publicly readable: Ministry of Sports data
- Confidence: H - Government-verified licensing data

**Netherlands (Dansersfonds / Nederlandse Dansdagen):**
- Claims: 200+ professional companies, limited school data
- Method: Project-based funding records
- Publicly readable: Annual reports
- Confidence: L - Professional sector only

## Critical Gaps & Contradictions

### Contradiction 1: Teacher Counts
- **Registry data (D3):** 15,000-20,000 registered businesses
- **Association claims:** 80,000-120,000 teachers
- **Gap:** 4-8x difference = freelancer undercount confirmed

### Contradiction 2: Market Size
- **Eurostat performing arts revenue:** €45B (EU27, 2021)
- **Dance-specific estimates:** €1.8-3.5B (4-8% of performing arts)
- **No methodology** to validate the 4-8% allocation

### Contradiction 3: Participation vs Consumption
- **Survey participation:** 8-15% adults/year
- **Ticket sales data:** €2.1B (European performing arts, 2022)
- **Dance share unknown** - no separate tracking

## Red Bull Application Relevance

### What's Usable:
1. **French licensing data** (300,000+ license holders) - most reliable
2. **German employment data** (47,000 creative arts) - official source
3. **Participation rate range** (8-15%) - consistent across surveys
4. **Festival count data** (243+ salsa/bachata festivals) - verifiable

### What's Not Usable:
1. **Total market size estimates** - no credible methodology
2. **Per-capita dancer figures** - no harmonized definition
3. **Growth projections** - all from paywalled sources
4. **School-level revenue** - no public financial data

## Methodological Recommendations
