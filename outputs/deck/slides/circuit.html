The artists are the network that connects a fragmented market: working with about 15 of them reaches four congresses in five.

Fragmentation is the obstacle we found: hundreds of independent organisers, where Hyrox has one. The line-ups show a way around it. We read the published line-ups of 61 congresses drawn at random: 452 different teachers and couples, most of them booked once. But a small group tours the whole circuit: the 14 most booked artists together appear at 80 percent of these congresses, and the top 10 at 61 percent. The most booked are Cristian and Gabriella, Kike and Nahir, Melvin and Gatica, Ronald and Alba. More than half of the most booked artists live in Spain. For a brand, these artists connect the market better than any single organiser. Limit: line-ups are published by about 40 percent of congresses, mostly on goandance, which leans to bachata and Spain, so the concentration may be lower for salsa.
