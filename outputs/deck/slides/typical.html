Same word, "event", two different businesses. We drew 30 events at random per world. The typical Latin event is a three-day congress of workshops and parties where the dancers themselves pay: in a second, larger sample of 150 congresses the median full pass is 110 euros (half of them between 88 and 133), about 31 euros per day; the most expensive we found is 250. Longer events cost little more, so the price per day falls with length. The typical breaking event is a one-day battle: a spectator pays about 9 euros, a competitor about 10. Both worlds teach (workshops are almost everywhere) and both compete, but national championships are small in both, between 46 and 125 competitors. So the Latin mass is not in competitions; it is in the congresses.
