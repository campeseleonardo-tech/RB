Attention, measured as Instagram followers. If you look only at the famous events, breaking looks bigger: its flagships are global touring brands with a median of 30,000 followers against 15,000 for Latin flagships, though with 20 events per side the difference is not statistically solid. If you draw events at random, the picture flips: the typical Latin congress has about 4,500 followers, the typical breaking battle about 1,600, and this difference is significant. The 30 most followed dancers reach the same audience in both worlds: 4.8 million followers in Latin, 4.1 million in breaking. Likes and comments could not be measured at scale: Instagram does not show them without logging in.
