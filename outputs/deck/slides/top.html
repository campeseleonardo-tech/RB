The top of each market. We checked the 35 largest candidates per world for a stated size. Latin has 10 events a year with more than 1,000 dancers taking part, about 25,000 dancers in total; the largest are the Berlin Salsacongress (around 6,000), the Croatian Summer Salsa Festival in Rovinj (5,000 to 6,500), Amsterdam (2,000 to 3,000) and Sofia (2,500). Breaking has one event with more than 1,000 breakers taking part, Outbreak Europe in Banska Bystrica (about 2,000). Breaking's large events are shows: the Notorious IBE in Heerlen with 10,000 visitors, the Battle Pro final in Paris with 4,500 planned seats, Outbreak with about 3,200. No Latin event states spectators. The figures are organisers' claims and minimums: 20 of 35 Latin and 15 of 35 breaking candidates publish no size at all. Juste Debout (up to 12,000 spectators) is left out because it has no breaking category; Red Bull events are excluded.
