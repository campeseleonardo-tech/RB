Hyrox is our check. It is a fitness race where people pay to take part, and Red Bull is visible on all 15 race pages we read, named as partner on 9. In 2025 Hyrox had 89 races worldwide and 779,073 finishers, about 8,750 per race; the largest, London ExCeL, had more than 34,000. In Europe it held 61 races in the last 12 months, 22 percent more than the year before, at a median price of 75 euros. Red Bull also shows up where people take part elsewhere: F45 studios, a dance zone at a music festival, free workshops in London. Compared with Hyrox, the Latin circuit has about ten times as many events, and dancers pay more and stay three days; the ten largest congresses average about 2,400 dancers. What Hyrox has and Latin lacks is one organiser: one partnership covers every race. That is the problem a Latin entry has to solve.
