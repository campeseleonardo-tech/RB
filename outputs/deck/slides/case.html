A light business case, computed from the figures already shown; it is a scenario, not a forecast. Ticket money per flagship event is of the same order in all three worlds: a Latin congress of 2,000 dancers at a 150 euro pass takes about 300,000 euros from its dancers; a breaking show of 10,000 spectators at 35 euros about 350,000; a Hyrox race about 656,000 from its athletes. What changes is who pays: dancers, spectators, athletes. For Red Bull the unit is the dancer-night, an adult dancing until late: one pilot congress is about 6,000 dancer-nights, the ten largest congresses about 75,000, and the whole circuit perhaps 180,000 entries a year if the typical event has 300 dancers, an assumption to confirm with organisers. At about 0.87 euros of Red Bull revenue per can, direct sales are small even at one can per night: the value is brand presence in a crowd Red Bull does not reach today.
