Leonardo Campese

This study compares two dance worlds in Europe: breaking, where Red Bull is the reference brand, and salsa and bachata, where no Red Bull presence was found. A third world, Hyrox fitness racing, is the check: a market where people pay to take part and Red Bull is already a partner. The colours stay the same on every slide: red for Latin, blue for breaking, khaki for Hyrox. Everything comes from public sources, and every number is traced in the project data with its source and date.
