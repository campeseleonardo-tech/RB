Be the energy of the nights

What

Product presence at congresses: night bar, energy partner of the socials, sampling, a Red Bull night with the headline artists

Reach

Beat fragmentation: the 10 congresses with 1,000+ dancers (25,000 dancers) and the 15 touring artists who appear at 80% of congresses

First step

One pilot congress in Paris (or Barcelona, the largest Latin scene), agreed with the organiser, measured: cans, contacts, dancers' feedback

Not a new competition: national finals have 65-123 competitors. Not paid influencers: dancers' reach shows where attention is.

15

The data point to a light entry. Latin congresses are long nights where adults dance until late: a natural place for an energy drink, the same logic as Hyrox and F45. So the proposal is presence, not a new property: a night bar, being the energy partner of the socials, sampling, perhaps a Red Bull night built around the artists who draw the crowd. To beat fragmentation, start with the ten congresses that each bring more than 1,000 dancers, about 25,000 together, and work with the small group of touring artists who appear at four congresses in five: they connect the circuit better than any organiser. First, one pilot congress in Paris, the top city in our ranking, or Barcelona, the largest Latin scene, with clear measures. No new Latin competition: national finals have only 65 to 123 competitors.
