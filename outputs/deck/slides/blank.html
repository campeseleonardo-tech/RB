Red Bull runs BC One and Dance Your Style across Europe: 9 of the 15 large cities we studied had at least one Red Bull dance event in 2024-2026 (the 15: Salzburg, Innsbruck, Vienna, Graz, Munich, Berlin, Zurich, Milan, Rome, Barcelona, Madrid, Paris, London, Amsterdam, Warsaw). For salsa and bachata we found nothing in nine countries (AT, DE, CH, IT, ES, FR, NL, UK, PL): no event, no sponsorship, no sampling. "Not found" means not on the pages and searches we read, since most congresses do not publish their partners. In media the gap is large: BC One has 1.21 million YouTube subscribers, twelve times the largest Latin competition channel (World Salsa Summit, 104,000). The question: is the blank a gap, or a market that simply does not fit Red Bull?
