Where would Red Bull start? We ranked 24 cities: the 15 we studied plus every city with at least eight Latin events in the census. Three equal blocks: the Latin scene (Latin events in the city over three years, congresses with more than 1,000 dancers within 150 kilometres, dance schools mapped in OpenStreetMap), Red Bull on the ground (its dance events 2024-2026, an office in the metro, Hyrox races), and reach (population, share of people aged 20 to 39, air passengers). Each indicator is a percentile among the 24 cities. Paris comes first, and stays in the top three under all 2,000 random weightings we tried; London and Amsterdam follow. Barcelona has by far the most Latin events, 50 in three years, but Red Bull's Spanish office is in Madrid; it is fourth. Salzburg is 21st: next to headquarters, but no Latin scene to speak of.
