Beyond events: how much dance content circulates. On Instagram, the #bachata tag holds about 38 million reels, #salsadance 3.1 million and #bachatadance 0.9 million, against 13 million for #breakdance; #bachata also includes music posts, so read it as an upper bound. On YouTube, the three most viewed Latin dance videos outside Red Bull competitions have 291, 99 and 42 million views (the first may be driven by the song); breaking's top three have 100, 91 and 51 million, mostly battles. Breaking leads clearly only in competition media, where Red Bull's own channels dominate. Engagement rates (likes, comments) are not shown: they are not readable without logging in.
