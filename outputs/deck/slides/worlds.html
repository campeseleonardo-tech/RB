03

Three worlds, always in the same colours. Latin is the case: salsa and bachata congresses, festivals and weekenders, the multi-day events dancers travel to. Weekly parties and classes are left out because they cannot be counted from public sources. Breaking is the reference, the dance world Red Bull knows; its events are battles, jams with a battle, championships and festivals with a breaking category. Hyrox is the check: a fitness race where people pay to take part, as in Latin, and where Red Bull is already a partner. Europe excludes Russia, Belarus and Turkey. Red Bull's own events are recorded but never used as the comparison, so that we compare markets, not Red Bull with itself.
