# Outreach kit (for Leonardo's talks)

Status 2026-10-21. What to send, what to ask, in what order. Attach or show `outputs/one_page.md` (the one-page summary). Keep every talk short: 20-30 minutes, no confidential data asked.

## 1. Order of the talks
1. **Two or three Latin congress organisers you know** (first: they give the facts Red Bull will ask for, and your network is strongest here).
2. **One breaking event organiser** (control: is breaking really "spectacle on top, small battles below"?).
3. **Two or three Red Bull contacts** (last: you go with a tested idea and the organisers' answers).

## 2. Message to a congress organiser (adapt the language)
> Hi [name], I am working on a small study on the Latin dance event market in Europe (salsa and bachata congresses compared with breaking events), from public data: about 250 events, prices, sizes, social following. I would like to check the results with a few organisers. Do you have 20 minutes for a call in the next two weeks? No confidential numbers needed; ranges are fine, and nothing is published with your name without your OK. I can share the summary in return.

## 3. Questions for congress organisers (ranges are fine)
1. How many people came to your last edition? How many dancers with a full pass, how many with party-only tickets?
2. Age range and share from abroad, roughly?
3. How many nights of parties, until what time? Is there a bar, and who runs it (you, the venue, a partner)?
4. Which brands sponsor or partner with you today? Any drinks brand? In exchange for what (money, product, visibility)?
5. Would you welcome an energy-drink partner at the socials (product, a bar, a branded night)? What would make it a yes or a no?
6. Where does your audience find you: Instagram, Facebook, goandance, other platforms, word of mouth?
7. How many similar events do you know in your country per year that are NOT listed on goandance?

## 4. Questions for a breaking organiser (control)
1. Last edition: how many competitors, how many paying spectators, workshop participants?
2. Ticket and entry prices; main income sources (tickets, sponsors, public funding)?
3. Sponsors today; do energy-drink brands take part, and how?
4. Where are breaking events listed? How many events in your country are not on and8.dance?

## 5. Message to a Red Bull contact
> Hi [name], I have been working on a project on dance markets in Europe: breaking, where Red Bull is strong, compared with salsa and bachata congresses, where it is not present. One page attached. Before going further I would like your view on whether the idea makes sense from inside, and who would be the right person to talk to. 20 minutes, whenever it suits you.

## 6. Questions for Red Bull contacts (no confidential data)
1. Does Red Bull do product presence or sampling at events where people take part (not watch), beyond fitness and dance zones? Who decides that: country marketing, events, a field marketing team?
2. What does Red Bull look for in such a partnership (audience age, nights, numbers, brand fit)? Is a 25-45 year old adult audience a fit?
3. How are these activations measured (cans, contacts, content)?
4. Has Latin dance ever been discussed internally, and if not, why do you think?
5. Who would be the right person to show this to, and in what form (one page, a short deck, a pilot proposal)?

## 7. After each talk
Write the answers (ranges, no names unless agreed) in research/manual_input.ods, sheet "Talks" (one row per answer), or send them to Claude in the chat: they become rows of data/ with `stated_by_organiser` or a note "interview".
