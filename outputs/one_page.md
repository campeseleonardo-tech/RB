# Can Red Bull enter Latin dance? (Dance Scene Atlas, one page)

**The problem.** Red Bull is strong in breaking, a spectator market. Salsa and bachata are the opposite: many people dance, pay and travel, and almost nobody watches the competitions. Red Bull has no visible presence there. Can it enter, how, and where?

**The market, counted** (census of the European event calendars, Nov 2024 - Oct 2027; random samples of 30 events per world; Red Bull's own events excluded from the comparisons).
- Latin: about 450-750 congresses and festivals a year in Europe, stable over two years; typical event 3 days, full pass about EUR 115 (up to 245), 4,461 Instagram followers; flagships with 1,000-5,000 paying dancers. Spain, Germany, Italy and France hold half of them; Barcelona is the largest Latin event city (1).
- Breaking: about 210 events a year on its main calendar, stable; a few global brands on top (Summer Dance Forever 283,000 followers, IBE 10,000 visitors) and a long tail of one-day battles: typical event 1,641 followers, ticket about EUR 9, entry fee about EUR 10 (2).
- The typical Latin event is more followed than the typical breaking event (p = 0.02); the 30 most followed dancers are equal (4.8 vs 4.1 million followers); competitions are small in both worlds (46-125 competitors per national final) (3).

**The reference: Hyrox.** A participation market where Red Bull is already a partner (named on all 15 race pages read): about 45-60 European races a year, growing fast, about EUR 75 per athlete. The Latin congress circuit has about ten times as many events, and its participants pay more and stay longer. The difference: Hyrox is one organiser; Latin is hundreds (4).

**How Red Bull could enter.** Product presence across congresses (night bar, energy partner of the socials, sampling, a Red Bull night with the headline artists), as at Hyrox, F45 and festival dance zones (5). Because the market is fragmented, start with the 20-30 largest congresses or a partner that reaches many at once. Not a new competition, and not sponsored dancers. No drinks brand except Freixenet was found at congresses; Red Bull appears at none (6).

**Where.** Barcelona and Paris first (largest Latin hubs with a Red Bull office or dance event and Hyrox races), then Madrid, Amsterdam and London. A first, cheap pilot could run near Red Bull's headquarters (an Austrian congress) (7).

**Next step.** One pilot: one congress activation, agreed with an organiser, measured (cans, contacts, dancers' feedback).

**How sure are we.** Sound: the media gap; the stable size and shape of the Latin congress market; Red Bull's role at Hyrox. Thin: typical event sizes (rarely published), sponsors, breaking seen through one calendar. Method: public sources only; every number is traced in outputs/analysis.md (sections 9-11) and data/.
