## Summary (max 200 words)

This table documents 10 major non-Red Bull hip hop/breaking events and 8 Caribbean/Latin social dance events across Europe, all verified as absent of Red Bull sponsorship. The hip hop/breaking cohort includes Juste Debout (Paris, >10,000 participants, annual), Battle of the Year (Dijon, annual since 1990), IBE (Heerlen, thousands, annual), Hip Hop International (world championship, 55+ countries annual), and Outbreak Europe (Slovakia, 2000 participants from 94 countries, annual). The Latin cohort comprises Berlin Salsa Congress (Berlin, ~6,000 participants from 60+ countries, annual), Amsterdam International Salsa Festival (Amsterdam, 2,000 from 17 countries, annual), Crazy 4 La Timba (Lisbon, 5th edition 2026), Croatian Summer Salsa Festival (Rovinj, 5,000+ from 90 countries, annual), and BCN Dance Life Bachata Congress (Barcelona, +1,000 attendees). Pilot cities Barcelona (bachata congress), Amsterdam (salsa festival), and Berlin (salsa congress) host events; Paris hosts Juste Debout as a reserve city. No Red Bull presence was verified for any listed event. Confidence ranges from H (Wikipedia-derived but cross-checked) to M (stated by organizers). Sources are all public-facing; no paywall bypass or platform scraping was required.
