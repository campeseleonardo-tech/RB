## Breaking
- Definition: Acrobatic street dance from Bronx, NY (1970s), featuring power moves, footwork, freezes, top rock
- Origin: Bronx, New York City, 1970-1980, African American and Puerto Rican youth
- Competition: 1v1 battles, best-of-three rounds (~60 seconds each), Olympic breaking uses "Trivium" judging
- Judging: **Contradiction between sources** - WDSF/Trivium says 5 criteria (technique, vocabulary, originality, execution, musicality) vs Olympic/Buzzing sources say 6 criteria (creativity, personality, technique, variety, performativity, musicality). Creativity/performativity weighted 60%
- Names: Breaking, Breakdance, B-boying, B-girling, B-Boying, Breakdancing (media term)
- Source URLs: https://www.olympics.com/en/sports/breaking, https://www.worlddancesport.org/Paris2024, https://www.nytimes.com/2024/08/10/briefing/breaking-olympics-explained.html
- Access date: 2026-10-19
- Confidence: High
- Notes: Olympic sport since 2024 Paris, excluded from 2028 LA Olympics. "Trivium" system developed by WDSF for Buenos Aires 2018 Youth Games

## Hip Hop Freestyle (New Style)
- Definition: Standing dance style incorporating hip hop culture elements, freestyle improvisation to hip hop/R&B music
- Origin: 1990s, MTV era, NYC mainstream exposure, evolved from old school foundations
- Competition: Freestyle battles, cyphers, Juste Debout hip-hop categories, all-style competitions
- Judging: Improvisation, musicality, technique, creativity, stage presence
- Names: Hip Hop, Hip Hop Freestyle, New Style, All Styles
- Source URLs: https://www.redbull.com/int-en/dance-your-style-different-styles-guide, https://www.alegria.dance/en/dances/hip-hop
- Access date: 2026-10-19
- Confidence: High
- Notes: Umbrella term encompassing many substyles, judged differently from breaking. Featured in Red Bull Dance Your Style competitions

## House
- Definition: Social dance to house music, featuring jacking, footwork, lofting elements
- Origin: Chicago & New York, early 1980s, house music birth
- Competition: 2v2 battles (Juste Debout), footwork battles, house categories
- Judging: Musicality, footwork complexity, individuality, connection to music
- Names: House Dance, House, Chicago House, New York House
- Source URLs: https://www.mygrooveguide.com/dance-info/house-dance/what-is-house-dance, https://juste-debout.com/en/home-page-en/
- Access date: 2026-10-19
- Confidence: High
- Notes: Three main elements: jacking (upper body), footwork (lower body), lofting (floor work). Chicago vs Detroit footwork rivalry

## Popping
- Definition: Funk-based dance using rapid muscle contractions (hits), robot/waving/tutting movements
- Origin: Fresno, California, late 1970s, evolved from boogaloo
- Competition: 2v2 battles, popping categories in Juste Debout, popping-specific competitions
- Judging: Musicality, precision of pops/hits, creativity, flow, animation quality
- Names: Popping, Animation, Boogaloo, Hit, Robot, Tutting, Waving
- Source URLs: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popping, https://www.kqed.org/news/11257343/the-hip-hop-dance-craze-born-in-fresno-popping
- Access date: 2026-10-19
- Confidence: High
- Notes: Electric Boogaloos popularized style. Michael Jackson heavily influenced by popping. Techniques include strobing, animation

## Locking
- Definition: Funk dance with sharp freezes (locks), isolations, robotic movements
- Origin: Los Angeles, late 1960s, Don Campbell invention
- Competition: 2v2 battles, locking categories in Juste Debout, locking competitions
- Judging: Musicality, precision of locks, creativity, timing, style
- Names: Locking, Campbellocking, Lock
- Source URLs: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locking_(dance), https://www.nytimes.com/2020/04/23/arts/dance/don-campbell-dead.html
- Access date: 2026-10-19
- Confidence: High
- Notes: Originally called Campbellocking, shortened to Locking. Related to popping but distinct - locking uses funk music vs popping uses funk/electro

## Krump
- Definition: Aggressive, energetic dance evolved from clowning, characterized by rapid-fire movements
- Origin: South Central LA, early 2000s, evolved from 1990s clowning
- Competition: 1v1/2v2 battles, Afro Descendant category in Juste Debout, krump battles
- Judging: Energy, intensity, originality, technical skill, connection to music
- Names: Krump, Clowning (predecessor), K.R.U.M.P. (acronym)
- Source URLs: https://www.nytimes.com/2005/06/19/magazine/the-clowning-wildingout-battle-dancers-of-south-central-la.html, https://krumpinc.com/history
- Access date: 2026-10-19
- Confidence: High
- Notes: Created by Ceasare "Tight Eyez" Willis and Jo'Artist "Big Mijo" Ratti. K.R.U.M.P. stands for Kingdom Radically Uplifted Mighty Praise

## Waacking
- Definition: Expressive dance with dramatic arm movements, poses, theatricality
- Origin: Los Angeles, 1970s, gay clubs, evolved from punking
- Competition: Experimental categories, not mainstream battles, sometimes in Juste Debout
- Judging: Musicality, dramatic expression, arm technique, theatricality, creativity
- Names: Waacking, Whacking, Punking (predecessor)
- Source URLs: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waacking, https://www.theguardian.com/stage/2021/jul/13/waacking-martial-arts-disco-dance-craze-resurfaced-asia-john-travolta-donna-summer
- Access date: 2026-10-19
- Confidence: Medium
- Notes: Originally punking, renamed waacking to remove negative connotations. Strong theatrical, Hollywood-inspired influence

## Vogue
- Definition: Highly stylized ballroom dance from Harlem, NYC (1960s-1980s), gender performance art
- Origin: Harlem, NYC, 1960s-1980s, Black/Latino LGBTQ ballroom culture
- Competition: Categories in ballroom scene (FF/MF), not mainstream hip-hop battles, vogue categories in Juste Debout sometimes
- Judging: Five elements: hand performance, catwalk, duckwalk, floor performance, spins/dips. Realness (gender performance) crucial
- Names: Vogue, Voguing
- Source URLs: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vogue_(dance), https://nmaahc.si.edu/explore/stories/brief-history-voguing
- Access date: 2026-10-19
- Confidence: Medium
- Notes: Three styles: Old Way (precision, symmetry), New Way (fluidity), Vogue Fem (exaggerated femininity). Gender performance central - FF/MF categories

## Recommendations: disciplines vs sub-styles (+ ID mapping)

### Core Hip Hop Disciplines ( treat as DISCIPLINE for grid):
- **URB-BRK** (Breaking) - Core discipline, Olympic status
- **URB-HIP** (Hip Hop Freestyle/New Style) - Core discipline
- **URB-HOU** (House) - Core discipline  
- **URB-POP** (Popping) - Core discipline
- **URB-LOC** (Locking) - Core discipline
- **URB-KRU** (Krump) - Core discipline

### Separate Disciplines ( treat as separate DISCIPLINE):
- **URB-WAA** (Waacking) - Recommend separate due to distinct origins (LGBT clubs), theatrical nature, different music (disco vs funk/hip-hop)
- **URB-VOG** (Vogue) - Recommend separate due to ballroom origins, gender performance focus, not hip-hop battles

### Investigate/Sub-Style Status:
- Afro styles - Current unclear if part of hip-hop family or separate. Project lists Afro as Juste Debout category but unclear taxonomy

### Dancehall Placement:
- Dancehall - **NOT part of hip-hop family** - Jamaican origin, separate cultural context, project currently lists under Latin/Caribbean with "family to be confirmed"

### Juste Debout Categories 2026:
- **Classic**: Popping, Locking, House Dance, Hip-Hop New Style (these map directly to core URB-* disciplines)
- **Afro Descendant**: Afro, Dancehall, Krump, Electro (Krump maps to URB-KRU, others need separate IDs)

## Summary (max 200 words)
Hip hop family includes seven core styles: Breaking (Olympic), Hip Hop Freestyle (New Style), House, Popping, Locking, Krump. Waacking and Vogue are theatrical/performance styles with distinct LGBT/ballroom origins that deserve separate discipline status rather than being sub-styles. Dancehall is Jamaican, not hip-hop family. Breaking uses "Trivium" judging (5-6 criteria). Juste Debout 2026 has Classic category (core 4 hip-hop styles) and Afro Descendant category (Afro, Dancehall, Krump, Electro). Recommendation: Core URB-BR*K, HIP, HOU, POP, LOC, KRU disciplines; separate URB-WAA, URB-VOG; Dancehall under CAR-DHL in Latin taxonomy. Afro styles need further investigation.
