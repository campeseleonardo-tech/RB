## Typical Constraints Reported Publicly

### Insurance Requirements:
- General liability insurance typically required for commercial teaching
- Risk coverage for dance activities (injury prevention)
- Studio-specific insurance requirements vary by location

### Noise Rules:
- Urban studios (Vienna, Munich, Berlin) have strict noise curfews
- Residential proximity restrictions (typically 10-22:00 hours)
- Sound equipment restrictions in some locations

### Opening Hours:
- Vienna: 8:00-22:00 (weekdays), 12:00-24:00 (weekends)
- Munich: Varies by location, often 8:00-22:00
- Berlin: Flexible but often limited by building regulations
- Salzburg: 7:00-21:00 daily

### Minimum Bookings:
- Vienna: 1-3 hours minimum common
- Munich: 4-8 hours for longer sessions
- Salzburg: 2 hours minimum for hourly rate
- Weekend bookings often require longer minimums (4-8 hours)

### Additional Constraints:
- **Registration Requirements**: Some studios require dance teacher certification
- **Equipment Requirements**: Many studios require own sound equipment
- **Background Checks**: Some institutions require criminal record checks
- **Dance Style Restrictions**: Some studios specialize in specific dance forms (salsa, hip-hop, etc.)
