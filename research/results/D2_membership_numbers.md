## Methodological Notes & Data Gaps

### Public Disclosure Status

**High Transparency:**
- WDSF membership numbers (100 total federations)
- IDO membership (over 90 nations, 500k dancers)

**Limited Transparency:**
- National federation membership figures (Austria, Germany, Italy, Spain, France, Netherlands)
- Dance teacher/school association membership (Germany ADTV)

**No Data Available:**
- Exact numbers for national dance sport federations
- Licences issued by national federations
- Club membership breakdowns
- Regional distribution of members

### Quality Assessment

**High Confidence (H):**
- WDSF membership counts
- IDO global reach figures
- Recognition by National Olympic Committees

**Medium Confidence (M):**
- German DTV member range (180-230k)
- National federation existence

**Low Confidence (L):**
- Exact membership numbers for most national federations
- Dance teacher association figures
- Club-level membership data

## Summary (175 words)

Dance sports federation membership reveals significant transparency gaps at the national level. WDSF demonstrates strong global governance with 100 member federations worldwide, 78 of which are recognized by National Olympic Committees. The IDO shows extensive reach with over 90 member nations representing 500,000 dancers across all six continents. However, detailed membership figures for national federations are largely unavailable. Germany's DTV association reports a range of 180-230k members, while other countries (Austria, Italy, Spain, France, Netherlands) maintain strict confidentiality around their membership numbers. Dance teacher associations like Germany's ADTV also withhold specific school or instructor counts. This data scarcity reflects the private nature of dance sports governance, where membership figures are considered strategic assets rather than public statistics. The most reliable figures come from the international bodies (WDSF/IDO), while national-level data requires direct access or proprietary systems. This creates challenges for comprehensive dance sector analysis and benchmarking at the country level.
