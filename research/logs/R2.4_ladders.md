# R2.4 - Ladders: where the national winner goes next (2026-10-20)

One line per country x discipline. Sources: relations.csv R000049-R000059 and the R2.1/R2.2 pages.

## Breaking (URB-BRK)
- AT: Austrian Breaking Championship (UDVOE, e.g. The Freshest POA 2025 Vienna) -> Team Austria for WDSF championships (inferred, M). Red Bull route: no BC One national final in AT in 2026; BC One Cypher Austria (Innsbruck 19 Apr) winners go to the World Final Toronto (Last Chance path).
- DE: German Breaking Championships (Die Finals; Dresden 2025 108 breakers, Hannover 2026 107) -> WDSF route via DTV (inferred, M). BC One: no German stop in the target cities beyond Berlin cypher (winners -> Last Chance -> World Final).
- IT: Campionati Italiani Assoluti Breaking (FIDS, Riccione Jan 2026) -> WDSF route NOT verified this pass. BC One Italy = LAquila cypher -> Last Chance -> World Final.
- ES: Campeonato de Espana de Breaking (FEBD, Compostela Street Festival 2026, date unread) -> WDSF route NOT verified. BC One Spain = Madrid cypher 15 Mar -> Last Chance -> World Final.
- FR: Championnat de France de Breaking 2026 (20 Jun, 125 breakers) -> WDSF route NOT verified. WDSF World Breaking Championships 2026 is IN Paris (4 Dec) - the top of the ladder lands in the country.
- NL: NK Breaking (NADB) -> NADB ranking -> WDSF EK/WK and World Games (M, explicit on nadb.nl). BC One NL = Rotterdam cypher.
- UK: Breaking GB National Championships (last edition 8 Sep 2024) -> WDSF European/World Championships + Youth Olympics (M, explicit on breakinggb.org). Private alternative: UK B-Boy Championships World Finals (Manchester, 16 Aug 2026).
- PL: Mistrzostwa Polski Breaking (PZST) -> WDSF World/European Championships via PZST nomination (H, explicit in the criteria document).
- CH: Swiss Breaking Championship -> Swiss national team -> international competitions (inferred, M). No BC One Swiss stop (BC One Switzerland = Lausanne cypher).

- DE: Deutsche DAT Meisterschaften Salsa/Bachata/Latino (Eisenberg 12 Sep 2026; Brechen 25 Oct 2025) -> no verified international rung (the DAT circuit is national; WSF German Championship Salsa exists as a separate title).

- ES: Campeonato de Espana Caribenos + the FEBD Caribenos ranking -> WDSF World Championships Caribbean Dances (H, explicit on febd.es; Alicante 9-12 Oct 2026). Private alternative: La Negra Dance Festival World Cup (Calpe, Oct 2026).

- NL: no national championship found -> no ladder.
- UK: no national championship found -> no ladder.
- PL: no national championship found (PZST MP is dancesport) -> no ladder. International IDO World Caribbean Championships were hosted in Janow Podlaski 21-22 Nov 2025.
- CH: no Swiss championship edition in 2025/2026 found (next SFBT Offene Schweizermeisterschaft 8 May 2027 Solothurn, with salsa categories) -> no active ladder.
