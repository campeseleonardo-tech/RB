## SUMMARY

- **Breaking**: National ladders exist in DE, IT, ES, NL, FR, UK (via separate body Breaking GB), with AT and CH cross‑border governed by DTV. Progression: Socials/Jams → Ranking Battles (Breaking Battle Series) → Local/Regional Battles → National Championships (GBC, Coupe de France, Italian Championships, etc.) → Red Bull BC One national qualifier → World Final.
- **Social Salsa/Bachata**: No dedicated national federations for social salsa/bachata exist in any of the nine countries. The scene operates through festival circuit and cross‑border WDSF events (e.g., Guadalajara World Championships, La Negra Festival). German (DE), Italian (IT), Spanish (ES), Dutch (NL) have national salsa/bachata championships via existing dancesport federations (DTV, FIDS, FEBD, NADB), but these are dancesport-focused, not social governance.
- **Relations**: DTV governs breaking for DE, AT, CH, and influences PL. Breaking GB governs UK separately. FFDance (France), FIDS (Italy), FEBD (Spain), NADB (Netherlands), PZST (Poland) are national governing bodies for breaking. No single national body governs social salsa/bachata; governance is through festivals and WDSF world championships.
- **Red Bull BC One**: Serves as the top rung (international) and national qualifier for breaking; DYS positioning not confirmed for any country.
